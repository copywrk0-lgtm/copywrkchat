Legal · Privacy

Privacy, explained without the fog.

This policy explains what personal information Copywrk collects, why we use it, whom we may share it with, and the choices available to you.

Effective: 22 July 2026 · Last updated: 22 July 2026

1. Who we are

Copywrk is an independent digital services studio. We design websites, AI receptionists, lead follow-up systems, and workflow automations for businesses. For privacy questions, contact copywrk0@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to provide, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, industry, project requirements, and messages. If you become a client, we may also receive information needed to scope, deliver, support, and bill for the agreed work.

Our hosting and service providers may automatically process basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, requested pages, and timestamps for security, reliability, and diagnostics.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and schedule strategy calls.
  • Prepare proposals and deliver requested services.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve our website and systems.
  • Send service-related communications and, where permitted, relevant follow-up.
  • Meet legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, and dispute-resolution requirements.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Forms and interactive demos

When enabled, our enquiry form may use Formspree to deliver submissions to our inbox. Formspree processes the submitted information on our behalf under its own terms and privacy practices.

The sample AI receptionist on this website is a browser-based demonstration. Copywrk does not intentionally retain the demo conversation. Do not enter confidential, medical, financial, or otherwise sensitive information into a demonstration.

5. When information may be shared

We may share information with providers that help us host the website, receive forms, communicate, store business records, or deliver a client project. We provide only the information reasonably needed for their role. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or during a genuine business reorganisation.

6. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, the client relationship, legitimate records, or legal requirements. We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may ask what information we hold about you, request correction or deletion, withdraw consent for future processing, or raise a grievance. Email copywrk0@gmail.com with enough detail for us to identify and respond to the request. We may need to verify your identity.

8. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. The revised effective date will appear at the top of this page.
