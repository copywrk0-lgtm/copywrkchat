Trust · Security

Practical protection, honestly described.

Security is a shared responsibility. This page describes our current approach without claiming certifications or guarantees we do not have.

Last updated: 22 July 2026

Secure delivery

We use reputable hosting and service providers, encrypted HTTPS connections for public web traffic, and access controls appropriate to the systems we manage. Project architecture and safeguards vary with the agreed scope and sensitivity of the information involved.

Data minimisation

We aim to collect and retain only the information reasonably needed to answer an enquiry, deliver a project, provide support, or meet legitimate record-keeping obligations. Clients should avoid placing unnecessary sensitive information in forms, demonstrations, prompts, or support messages.

Access and providers

Access to project systems should be limited to people who need it. Some functionality relies on third-party providers—for example hosting, forms, telephony, messaging, AI models, databases, or automation platforms. Each provider has its own security responsibilities and terms.

AI-agent safeguards

For deployed AI systems, we recommend clear permissions, restricted tool access, logging appropriate to the use case, human escalation, testing before launch, and additional review for sensitive decisions. AI outputs should not automatically control high-impact medical, legal, financial, safety, or employment decisions without suitable human oversight.

Client responsibilities

  • Protect account credentials and use multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Grant only the access required and remove access when no longer needed.
  • Keep devices, integrations, content-management systems, and third-party accounts updated.
  • Tell us promptly about suspicious activity, compromised credentials, or unintended data exposure.
  • Obtain required permission for call recording, messaging, and personal-data processing.

No absolute guarantee

No website, automation, or connected service can be guaranteed completely secure or continuously available. We work to reduce reasonable risks and respond responsibly when an issue is reported.

Report a vulnerability

If you believe you found a security issue affecting a Copywrk-controlled website or system, email copywrk0@gmail.com with the affected URL, steps to reproduce, and potential impact. Please do not access, alter, download, or disclose other people’s data; disrupt services; use automated destructive testing; or publish details before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate.
